Our Story

Small space,
big flavors

Nestled inside the Rubis Service Station on Jomo Kenyatta Avenue, Café Levant is Mombasa's best-kept secret. We serve exceptional coffee, handcrafted drinks, and satisfying meals in a clean, air-conditioned space designed for calm.

Whether you're stopping for a morning espresso or a late-night burger, we're here — 24 hours, every day.
